Privacy

How we handle your details.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

WPG is design software for CNC panel products, developed by Ergolife. For any privacy question, write to inegvin@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into the early-access form: your email address and, if you add it, your shop name. We also note the date of the sign-up and the website address it came from.

Why we collect it

To invite you to try WPG when early access opens, and to send you important news about that access. We do not send advertising from other companies and we never sell or share your details.

Where it is stored

Sign-ups are saved in a private Google Sheet that only the WPG team can open, using Google's services (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script). This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts; like any website host, they may see your IP address when you open the page.

How long we keep it

Until WPG launches and early access ends, or until you ask us to delete it — whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your details, or to stop contacting you. Just write to inegvin@gmail.com and we will do it promptly.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking tools.
